Five-step walkthrough

  1. Check the latest published edition against the selected operational edition, source SHA and artifact evidence.
  2. Refresh state. An unavailable source leaves saved evidence in place; a successful source check does not prove every domain is current.
  3. Open Investigate & review. Choose an area, search, select edition/date/status filters, then inspect a record and its provenance.
  4. Submit a public review. All eight proposal states are supported. Applied requires a verified change to a known target. Save returns a revision; conflicts preserve your draft until you reload and reconcile.
  5. Use manual controls to open existing GitHub workflows. Enter the selected edition in GitHub. GitHub permissions govern production execution. Force Replace is prohibited through September 23, 2026.

Public reviews

Anything submitted here is public. Do not enter credentials, account/payment information, or personal information about another person. New authors are anonymous. Reviews append to history and do not change published stories or dispatch workflows.

Reading evidence

Unknown is not zero. Empty results mean no matching authoritative records were returned. Inspect availability, source timestamps, measurement boundary and source identity. Historical imported data has a fixed collection time; it is not a live audience counter. Exact evidence exposes the selected record with its source and digest.

Usage and audience

Usage windows combine only recorded numeric observations under compatible boundaries. Audience reports contain aggregate dimensions, collection metadata and exclusions, without individual identities. Truncated sources are labeled partial.

Schedules and recovery

The Publisher 07:00 and Validation/Repair 09:00 America/Chicago schedules are not created and not enabled. No cutover is performed. Refresh only reads and reconciles evidence. Recover a failed production stage through its canonical GitHub run; do not rerun completed editorial work.

Keyboard and mobile

Use Tab to reach controls, Enter to activate them, Escape to close record details, and the skip link to reach main content. Wide evidence tables scroll inside their containers.
